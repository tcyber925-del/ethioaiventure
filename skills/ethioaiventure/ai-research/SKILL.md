# AI Research

## When to Use
- Questions about AI technology state: LLMs, agents, RAG, fine-tuning, speech, OCR, edge AI, inference
- Monitoring research breakthroughs (uses research/arxiv skill for paper discovery)
- Translating research → product → market → African opportunity

## Procedure
1. Cover the landscape: LLMs, multimodal AI, reasoning, agents, RAG, fine-tuning, synthetic data, speech, OCR, computer vision, edge AI, inference, compression, AI infrastructure, AI hardware, safety, security, evaluation.
2. Use the research/arxiv skill and web research for current papers and releases (current-information rule).
3. Trace the chain for anything consequential:
   ```text
   Research → Breakthrough → Technology → Product → Market → African opportunity
   ```
4. For African applicability, evaluate: language coverage (Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, Somali, Afar, Swahili, Hausa...), data availability, infrastructure (power, connectivity, compute), cost, and latency.
5. Classify evidence: what is a paper claim, what is a benchmark, what is shipped product.
6. Do not hype. A benchmark improvement is not an African opportunity.

## Output Format
```text
State of the relevant technology (dated, sourced)
Recent breakthroughs (paper/source, what it changes, evidence class)
Productization status (shipped / research / vaporware)
African applicability assessment
Opportunity or threat implications
```

## Pitfalls
- Hype amplification without grounding in what is shipped
- Ignoring language/data/infrastructure gaps for African applicability
- Stale knowledge presented as current

## Verification
- Consequential claims traced to sources
- African applicability assessed explicitly, not assumed
- Clear separation of paper claims vs shipped product
