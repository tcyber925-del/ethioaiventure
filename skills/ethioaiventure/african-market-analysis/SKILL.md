# African Market Analysis

## When to Use
- Questions spanning multiple African countries
- Expansion analysis: "should we expand to X?", "which market first?"
- Any analysis that must not treat Africa as one market

## Procedure
1. Never treat Africa as one market. Segment by country, then by city and income tier.
2. Compare systematically across:
   - Purchasing power (GDP per capita, disposable income, USD/ETB/local currency dynamics)
   - Digital infrastructure (connectivity, smartphone penetration, compute)
   - Market maturity (adoption curves, existing players)
   - Regulations (data, fintech, telecom, sector-specific)
   - Payments (M-PESA, Telebirr, cards, cash, bank rails)
   - Distribution (channels, logistics, last-mile)
   - Language (working languages, local languages, literacy)
   - Enterprise structures (formal vs informal, procurement)
   - Competition (local, regional, international)
   - Capital (local VC, DFIs, diaspora)
   - Talent (cost, availability, quality)
3. For each market, produce a verdict on fit for the specific opportunity (not generic attractiveness).
4. Apply evidence discipline; verify claims per market.
5. Tier priorities: Ethiopia (Tier 1) → East Africa (Tier 2) → broader Africa (Tier 3).

## Output Format
```text
Market-by-market comparison table
Per-market fit verdict for the opportunity
Recommended entry order with reasoning
What evidence would change the recommendation
```

## Pitfalls
- "Africa is a country" analysis
- Using one market's success to claim another's (Nigeria ≠ Kenya ≠ Ethiopia)
- Ignoring currency and payment rails per market

## Verification
- At least 3-5 markets compared with sourced data
- Recommendation is opportunity-specific, not generic
- Assumptions exposed per market
