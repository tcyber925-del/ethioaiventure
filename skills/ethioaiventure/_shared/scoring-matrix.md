## Verdict

**Overall Score /100**

## Rules

1. The score never replaces reasoning — every dimension has a written justification.
2. Evidence class and confidence accompany every score (see evidence-classification.md).
3. Red-team the score: what would change it, and what evidence would you need?
4. Verdict buckets: BUILD (70+ with strong evidence), TEST (50-69, validate first), WATCH (30-49, track), AVOID (<30 or fatal flaw).
