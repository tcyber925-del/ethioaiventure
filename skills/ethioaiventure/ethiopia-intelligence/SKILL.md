# Ethiopia Intelligence

## When to Use
- Any question specifically about Ethiopia: AI companies, startups, government, universities, telecom, finance, agriculture, healthcare, education, logistics, manufacturing, construction, energy, tourism, professional services
- Ethiopia policy/regulation questions (deep-dive with policy-regulation)
- Weekly ecosystem report's Ethiopia section (cron job attaches this skill)

## Procedure
1. Scope the sector and time horizon. Ethiopia is Tier 1 — apply the deepest treatment.
2. Research the sector across: AI companies, startups, government initiatives, universities and research, telecom (Ethio Telecom, Safaricom Ethiopia), finance (banks, fintech, digital payments like Telebirr, Chapa, M-PESA), agriculture, healthcare, education, logistics, manufacturing, construction, energy, tourism, professional services.
3. Include local-language sources (Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, Somali, Afar) as evidence — news, government announcements, forums — applying source-evaluation.md.
4. Map digital infrastructure and regulatory context for the sector.
5. Recommendations must reflect Ethiopian realities: purchasing power, payments, connectivity, currency constraints (forex/ETB), power reliability, distribution, language.
6. Use fresh research for current state; verify consequential claims.

## Output Format
Sector-specific intelligence brief with: landscape, key players (verified), government/policy signals, infrastructure, capital, talent, research, opportunities, risks, unknowns, recommended actions.

## Pitfalls
- Importing Silicon Valley assumptions (cost structures, adoption curves, payment rails)
- Ignoring Ethiopian languages and local sources
- Treating Addis Ababa as the whole country — segment by market
- Unverified claims about government initiatives or funding

## Verification
- Ethiopia-specific sources used, not just global coverage
- Currency/economics expressed realistically (ETB purchasing power)
- Unknowns listed explicitly
