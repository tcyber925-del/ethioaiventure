# Customer Discovery

## When to Use
- Defining who to sell to and how to validate demand
- Designing interview/first-contact questions
- Before product strategy or MVP design

## Procedure
1. Identify:
   - ICP (ideal customer profile) — who, where, how many, in this economy
   - Buyer (pays) vs user (uses) vs decision maker (approves) — never confuse
   - Pain: severity, frequency, cost, emotional weight
   - Workflow: how the job is done today, step by step
   - Alternatives: current tools, manual work, doing nothing
   - Switching costs: what it costs to change
   - Procurement: budgets, cycles, approval chains, formal vs informal
   - Willingness to pay: what they pay today for alternatives, in local currency
2. Design discovery experiments, each with:
   ```text
   Hypothesis
   Questions
   Experiment
   Success Metric
   Failure Threshold
   Decision
   ```
3. Write non-leading questions. No "would you pay for X?" without grounding in current behavior.
4. Use validation-experiments.md-style discipline for the experiment design.

## Output Format
```text
ICP definition
Buyer / User / Decision maker map
Pain and workflow analysis
Alternatives and switching costs
Procurement reality
Willingness to pay evidence
Discovery experiment design
```

## Pitfalls
- Leading questions ("Wouldn't it be great if...")
- Talking to users who don't pay
- Assuming willingness to pay from stated interest

## Verification
- Each experiment has a falsifiable hypothesis, success metric, and failure threshold
- Questions grounded in current behavior, not hypotheticals
- Buyer/user/decision-maker separated explicitly
