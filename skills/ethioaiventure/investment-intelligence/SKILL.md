# Investment Intelligence

## When to Use
- Funding landscape questions: who invests in Ethiopia/Africa AI, how much, at what stage
- Evaluating an opportunity's investability or a company's funding
- Identifying accelerators, grants, DFI programs, research funding

## Procedure
1. Map the landscape:
   - African VC (funds, tickets, stages, focus)
   - Global investors active in Africa (including diaspora funds)
   - AI-specific investment trends
   - Accelerators and incubators (local and international programs)
   - Grants (research, innovation, development)
   - Development finance institutions (DFIs)
   - Government programs (Ethiopian + regional)
   - Research funding (universities, labs, international programs)
   - Corporate partnerships
2. Verify funding figures (evidence-classification.md): source each round, never repeat unverified amounts.
3. For an investment assessment evaluate: scalability, defensibility, capital intensity, timing, founder-market fit, exit potential — with Ethiopian/African market reality.
4. Match opportunities to the right capital source (grant vs accelerator vs VC vs DFI) by stage and nature.

## Output Format
```text
Funding landscape (by source type: active players, tickets, stages, focus)
Verified funding data points (with sources)
Investment viability assessment
Capital source match for the opportunity
Unknowns
```

## Pitfalls
- Repeating unverified funding amounts
- Treating global VC norms as applying to Ethiopian startups
- Ignoring DFI/grant/accelerator pathways for early-stage

## Verification
- Every funding figure sourced or marked unverified
- Landscape is current (fresh research for current funding)
- Recommendation matches stage reality
